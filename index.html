About

I'm a PhD candidate in computer science at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), advised by Professor Claudia Eckert, and a security researcher at Fraunhofer AISEC. Since September 2026, I'm a visiting scholar at UC Berkeley's Sky Computing Lab, working with Professor Natacha Crooks.

I build secure systems from applied cryptography and distributed trust, with a focus on anonymity and accountability. My main project, DACTIE, is a decentralized network for sharing cyber threat intelligence in which senders stay anonymous unless a threshold of archives jointly opens their group signature. At Berkeley, I contribute to a Lean 4 library for machine-checked cryptographic proofs with agentic AI.

My background in security operations, incident response and penetration testing keeps my research aimed at systems that can be deployed. I hold an M.Sc. in Cybersecurity from HDBW Munich and a B.Sc. in Information Systems from Hochschule Mainz.

Research

  • DACTIE: Decentralized Anonymous CTI Exchange. A permissioned, decentralized network for sharing threat intelligence with revocable anonymity: a sender stays anonymous unless a threshold of archives jointly opens its group signature. DACTIE composes GossipSub, Messaging Layer Security and threshold dynamic group signatures; the Rust prototype includes the first public implementation of the signature scheme. short version long version code
  • AI4Crypto (UC Berkeley, with Natacha Crooks). A Lean 4 library for automated, machine-checked cryptographic proofs with agentic AI; cryptographers and AI agents use it as a harness to specify new primitives and schemes.
  • EVulCoF: Evaluation of Vulnerability Communication Flows. A database of reported vulnerabilities and security advisories that uses LLMs to map vulnerability identifiers (CVE, PURL) to ecosystems and software versions.
  • PRESTO: Privacy Research in Smart home Environments with Time series Optimization. A real-world smart-home testbed whose data we prepare for publication as differentially private synthetic time series.
